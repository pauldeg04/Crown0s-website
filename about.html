About Crown Head Spa

Our story, our specialty

A closer look at where Crown Head Spa came from, and what makes our approach to head spa care different.

Our Beginning

Founded on the belief that self-care starts at the scalp

Crown Head Spa was established in June 2025 in Biñan, with a vision to provide a premium, relaxing, and intentional spa experience. We are a Christ-centered business, built on care and stewardship.

Thanks to the success of our Biñan branch, we opened our second location on May 10, 2026, in Calamba, Laguna — bringing the same standard of care to more guests across the province.

Our specialty is head spa treatment — a ritual combining deep scalp cleansing, therapeutic massage, steam, and premium hair care, crafted into an experience that's uniquely Crown.

Our Core Belief

Care begins with intentional stewardship

We do things with excellence and intention — because in the short time we have, we choose to do it well.

Our Mission

Restoring wellness, one scalp at a time

To provide every client with a premium, relaxing, and memorable wellness experience — using authentic techniques, attentive therapists, and a calm environment that puts their wellness first.

Our Vision

The trusted name in head spa care across the Philippines

To be the best head spa brand — known for consistent quality, continuous improvement, and a team that grows together.
