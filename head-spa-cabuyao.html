A head spa within reach of Cabuyao

As a neighboring city, Cabuyao is one of the closest places to our Calamba branch. Drop by for a scalp ritual after work, or pair a head spa with a body massage for a longer reset — we are open daily until 10:00 PM.

How to get here

Our branch at Dynasty Building, Brgy. Halang, Calamba is along the highway going to Pansol and Los Baños, opposite Puregold Halang. If you are coming via SLEX, exit at Turbina — the branch is about 15 minutes from the exit.

Parking: up to 8 shared slots with our co-tenants, plus the supermarket across the road where you can also park if our slots are full.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to Crown Head Spa from Cabuyao?

Our Calamba branch at Dynasty Building, Brgy. Halang is along the highway going to Pansol and Los Baños. If you are coming via SLEX, exit at Turbina — the branch is about 15 minutes from the exit.

Is there parking at Crown Head Spa Calamba?

Yes. There are up to 8 shared parking slots with our co-tenants, and the supermarket across the road also allows parking in case our slots are full.

Do I need to book ahead?

Walk-ins are welcome depending on availability, but we recommend booking online. No deposit is needed — you only pay at the spa.

Do you have a branch in Cabuyao?

No. Our two branches are in Biñan and Calamba, Laguna.
